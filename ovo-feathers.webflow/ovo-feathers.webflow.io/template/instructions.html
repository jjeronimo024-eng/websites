Step-by-Step Guide

Welcome, we understand that Webflow can be a bit confusing, this guide will help you get started. So grab a pen and paper and let's get started!

Figma file or more help?

If you have any questions or need additional assistance, please do not hesitate to reach out. We are here to help and support you every step of the way. Change Colors

1. Locate Color Variables (Left Menu)
2. Edit Any Color

‍Example
‍Want a darker theme? Change the Background Color to black and the Font Color to white. Want a fresh accent? Update the Blue variable to your brand’s color.

Change Iphone Image

1. Locate "Iphone Screen" (1) Class on the navigator
2. Go to the gear icon (2)
3. Change the image to whatever you want (3)
