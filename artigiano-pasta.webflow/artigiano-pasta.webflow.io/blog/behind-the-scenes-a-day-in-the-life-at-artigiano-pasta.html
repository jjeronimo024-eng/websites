Behind the Scenes: A Day in the Life at Artigiano Pasta

June 24, 2024

At Artigiano Pasta, every day is a celebration of authentic Italian culinary artistry. Join us for a glimpse behind the scenes to see how we craft our beloved handmade pasta.

‍

Early Morning: The Ingredients

Our day begins early as we source the finest ingredients. Fresh, locally-sourced eggs, high-quality durum wheat semolina, and seasonal vegetables are selected with care. The quality of our ingredients is the cornerstone of our pasta’s exceptional taste.

‍

Mid-Morning: The Dough

By mid-morning, our team is hard at work mixing and kneading the dough. This process is both an art and a science, requiring skilled hands and a deep understanding of traditional techniques. The dough is then allowed to rest, developing the perfect texture for shaping.

‍

Afternoon: Shaping the Pasta

After the dough has rested, it's time to shape it into various pasta forms. Whether it's delicate strands of tagliatelle, sturdy rigatoni, or intricate ravioli, each piece is crafted with precision. Our artisans use time-honored methods, ensuring that every batch maintains our high standards.

‍

Late Afternoon: Drying and Packaging

Once shaped, the pasta is carefully dried to preserve its quality. This slow drying process enhances the flavor and texture. Finally, the pasta is packaged, ready to bring a taste of Italy to your table.

‍

Evening: Preparing for the Next Day

As the day winds down, we prepare for tomorrow. Our team cleans and organizes the workspace, ensuring everything is ready for another day of pasta making.

‍

Conclusion: Crafting Excellence Daily

At Artigiano Pasta, every day is dedicated to crafting pasta with passion and precision. From sourcing the finest ingredients to meticulously shaping each piece, we take pride in every step of the process. Experience the art of authentic pasta with every bite.
