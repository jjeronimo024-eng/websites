Storing & Cooking

Are your products suitable for vegetarians?

We use plant-based ingredients in our vegan options and do not include any animal products such as meat, dairy, or eggs.

How long can I store pasta?

You can store our pasta for up to 1 year in a cool, dry place. Once opened, consume within 3-5 days for best freshness.

How long does it take to cook pasta?

Generally, pasta cooks in boiling water for 8-12 minutes.
