Cooking Instructions

How to Cook

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a rolling boil.
  2. Add the pasta and cook according to package instructions, stirring occasionally.
  3. Taste a piece to ensure it is al dente (firm to the bite).
  4. Drain the pasta and serve with your favorite sauce.
